The store

A shop that only makes sense
inside this building.

Faneuil Hall Marketplace has sold things to people passing through since 1826. We are part of that: a small store in the North Market Building, packed floor to ceiling, with a staircase in the middle leading to more of it.

Downstairs is gifts, glassware and the small things that travel well. Upstairs is apparel and sport. Ask anyone behind the counter where to eat in the market — they will tell you, and they will be right.

Where we are

In the middle of the oldest shopping street in the country.

Faneuil Hall Marketplace runs three long buildings deep. Quincy Market holds the food; North and South Market hold the shops. We are in North Market, on the Freedom Trail, a few minutes from the Old State House and the harbour.

The marketplace started America’s first retail pushcart programme here in 1976. Street performers still work the cobblestones every day from 11am.

On the Trail Faneuil Hall is stop nine of sixteen on the Freedom Trail.
Three stations State, Haymarket and Government Center are each a short walk.
Open late Nine at night, Monday through Saturday.
